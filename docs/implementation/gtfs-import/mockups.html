    One row per service day

    Default date: the typical service nearest the middle of the feed, never a date with an exception. "typical" changes to "chosen" when the planner picks another. Counts are editable; level C shows the Scenario's counts beside the proposal and changes nothing.

    Day groups, per service day

  • 4
    Part-year routes

    Closed when there are none. Each flagged route may get its own date, and optionally its own counts.

Decisions asked of you

A. Approve the five steps and the after-import changes as drawn, or name changes.
B. Days step: holidays open by default when there are any; day groups and part-year routes closed unless they find something. Agree?
C. Pattern names come from headsigns, so route 31 gets "Evans", "Evans 2" and "Evans 3". Proposal: when patterns of one direction share a headsign, name them by their last timepoint instead of a number, for example "Evans (to S Federal & Mississippi)". Agree?
D. Timepoint review lives on step 5, per pattern. The alternative is a separate step 5 "Timepoints" before Review (six steps). Keep it on Review?
