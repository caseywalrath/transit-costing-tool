  1. 1
    Fifth tab

    It covers the whole Project, so it ignores the selected Route (G-V9). The page title reads "Compare — RTD Nov 2026 vs Network Redesign v2".

  2. 2
    Two sides

    Each side is a Scenario, a Trip profile and a Blocking scenario (G-V1). The Blocking scenario defaults to the one Costing uses. ⇄ swaps the sides, and the signs flip.

  3. 3
    Day

    One switch for both sides. Custom days (for example "Holiday 2") appear here when either side has them. A day only one side has shows "—" on the other.

  4. 4
    Rates and counts

    Both default to the baseline's (G-V3, G-V4). The options are "Baseline's", "Proposed's" and "Each side's own". The rate shown is the first service year's.

  5. 5
    Totals

    Each tile shows baseline → proposed, with the change and percent underneath. Cost is for the chosen day over a year (daily hours × that day's annual count × rate).

  6. 6
    Table tools

    Filter by status, sort, and find. Download CSV writes every route for the chosen day, including unchanged ones (Frame 4).

  7. 7
    Change bar

    The bar's length is the size of the hours change, scaled to the largest row (mock-up question A).

  8. 8
    Unattributed

    Blocks that serve several routes, as in Costing. The row is always listed so the totals add up.

  9. 9
    Unchanged routes folded

    Clicking the row opens the list (mock-up question B). Its totals are shown, so you can check them.

Questions A–C: A. Keep the change bar? B. Fold unchanged routes? C. Sort by the largest hours change by default? Each is recommended yes.
