This trip can't reach Federal-32nd at exactly 8:16.

Travel times get longer when AM Peak starts at 8:00. A trip that starts at 7:59 reaches Federal-32nd at 8:14. A trip that starts at 8:00 reaches it at 8:18. No start time in between lands on 8:16.

Times a trip can reach Federal-32nd
8:148:168:18

To fix it, choose one in the Action list:

  • Keep a nearby time, 8:14 or 8:18. The trip moves 2 minutes either way.
  • Keep the start time (7:48) instead. Federal-32nd's time will change.
  • Skip this trip and leave it as it is.

Or make the jump smaller in Runtimes. In AM Peak, travel takes 1:00 longer from Westminster Station to Clear Creek and 2:00 longer from Clear Creek to Federal-32nd than in Early.

Trips that need attention are not changed until you choose a fix or skip them.
  • The Status chip says that something is wrong in two to four words. ⓘ opens the "Why?" panel on click or keyboard focus, not on hover alone.
  • The primary button stays disabled while any trip needs attention. "Skip all 3 that need attention" or a fix on each row enables it, and the button then counts the trips it will change.
  • A warning (the yellow "Layover too short") does not block the button.
  • Changes are shown in minutes ("+2 min"), not as before → after pairs. The exact before and after times are in each cell's title.

  • The order runs from the smallest change to the largest, and Skip is always last.
  • For "Two start times fit", the list reads: "Start at 7:52 (Early)", "Start at 7:55 (AM Peak)", "Keep Federal-32nd at 8:29 instead (2 min earlier)", and so on. For a profile that uses one time period per trip, choosing a start time picks that period.
  • For "No runtimes for this day", the list has only "Skip this trip". The panel says: "This pattern has no runtimes for Weekday. Assign a runtime profile in Runtimes, or skip this trip." with an Open runtimes button.
